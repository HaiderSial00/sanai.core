📜 Sanai Lore

The stories, moments and legendary incidents that became part of Sanai history.

SPORTS DAY • 7TH CLASS

The Broken Window Incident 🏏💥

It was Sports Day morning, around 10 AM. Admin and three boys from 10 Lime were on the top floor playing cricket. Admin was batting while one of the 10 Lime boys was bowling. Then came that one shot — the ball flew toward the class next to the lab and... CRASH. 💥 The window glass broke. After that, Sir Liaquat went on a legendary search for the culprit. But thanks to the ultimate bhai-chara, the mystery somehow stayed a mystery. 😭😂

7TH CLASS • ELECTION SEASON

The Election We Almost Won 🗳️😭

Back in 7th class, school elections had everyone busy with campaigns. Admin and his fellow admin went all in, even taking down their opponent's posters one morning. 😭 They teamed up with Head Boy Huzaifa and pushed the campaign hard. But after all that effort... they lost by just 13 votes to the guy from 7 Blue. 💀

8TH CLASS • EXAM MYSTERY

The Invigilator Mystery 👀📚

The first exam had the usual setup. Then, somehow, by the second paper, the room had a completely different invigilator. There were only a few students in that room and the atmosphere became... strangely cooperative. 😭 The Islamiat examination department never figured out what really happened. The marks came back looking pretty good — and the mystery invigilator became one of Sanai's strangest unsolved stories. 👀📜
