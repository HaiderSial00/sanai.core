📜 Sanai Lore

The stories, moments and legendary incidents that became part of Sanai history.

SPORTS DAY • 7TH CLASS

The Broken Window Incident 🏏💥

It was Sports Day morning, around 10 AM. While everyone was busy with the day's activities, Admin and three boys from 10 Lime were on the top floor playing cricket. Admin was batting while one of the 10 Lime boys was bowling. Then came that one shot — the ball flew toward the class next to the lab and... CRASH. 💥 The window glass broke. After that day, Sir Liaquat went on a legendary search for the culprit. But thanks to the ultimate bhai-chara, the mystery somehow stayed a mystery. 😭😂

7TH CLASS • ELECTION SEASON

The Election We Almost Won 🗳️😭

Back in 7th class, school elections had everyone busy with their campaigns. Admin and his fellow admin teamed up and went all in. One morning, they even took down the posters of their opponent from 7 Blue — full campaign mode. 😭 They teamed up with Head Boy Huzaifa, did the collab, pushed the campaign everywhere and gave it everything they had. But after all that effort... the final result came in: they lost by just 13 votes to the guy from 7 Blue. 💀 A full campaign, a Head Boy collab, maximum effort — and 13 votes said otherwise.

8TH CLASS • EXAM MYSTERY

The Invigilator Mystery 👀📚

The first exam had the usual setup: a maths teacher was invigilating. Then, somehow, by the second paper, the room had a completely different invigilator. There were only a few of us in that room, and somehow the whole exam atmosphere became... suspiciously cooperative. 😭 By the end of the Islamiat examinations, the department never figured out what had really happened in that room. The marks came back looking pretty good — and the identity of the mystery invigilator remained one of Sanai's strangest unsolved stories. 👀📜
